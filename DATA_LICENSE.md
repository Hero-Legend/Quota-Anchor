# Dataset terms and provenance

The MIT License in `LICENSE` applies to repository code only. It does not
replace the terms of upstream data providers.

## CTD-derived benchmark annotations

The CTD query labels, relation provenance, and candidate-source metadata are
derived from the Comparative Toxicogenomics Database (CTD). CTD uses custom
terms and is free for non-commercial research and educational use; commercial
use may require a separate licence. Users must review and comply with the
current CTD terms before using these files. This repository does not include a
CTD report download.

Source: https://ctdbase.org/reports/

## CIViC

The frozen CIViC evidence snapshot and the derived CIViC query annotations are
released under the Creative Commons Public Domain Dedication, CC0 1.0
Universal, consistent with CIViC's published data policy.

Source: https://civicdb.org/ and https://docs.civicdb.org/

## PubMed

The repository redistributes PubMed identifiers and publication years only.
It intentionally excludes article titles and abstracts. Abstracts are supplied
by publishers and may remain copyrighted. Users reconstruct citation text from
NCBI services under the applicable NCBI and publisher terms.

Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/download/

## PheKnowLator

The historical-firewall annotations record ontology-projected absence from the
November 2021 PheKnowLator source state. The original PheKnowLator graph is not
redistributed here; users should obtain the corresponding release from its
official project repository.
